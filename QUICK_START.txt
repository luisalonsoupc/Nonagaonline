🎯 GAME RULES SUMMARY:

Setup:
- 19 hexagonal discs on board
- 6 pieces: 3 Red, 3 Black (interleaved at corners)
- Red player goes first

Each Turn:
1. Move one of YOUR pieces in a hexagonal direction
   (moves until hitting another piece or board edge)
2. Move one outer disc to a new position
   (must touch at least 2 other discs)

Win:
- First player to connect all 3 pieces adjacent
- Adjacent = touching each other in any configuration
- Can form line, triangle, or curve
